Check how I [organized a networking meeting in Bochum](/2026/06/12/our-waste-in-motion-research.html), with lots of excursions.

Note also my [e-waste research](/2021/11/20/ewaste-dissertation-research.html).

Waste happens to be my favourite topic for public engagement: in theater, public discussions, keynotes, and radio shows. Always happy for invites.
