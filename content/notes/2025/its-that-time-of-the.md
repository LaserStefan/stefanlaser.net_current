It's that time of the year again in #Hanoi, #Vietnam, when the capital is planetary top of the flops, air-wise. 

My outdoor #airpollution sensor records numbers beyond insanity. The temperature inversion traps particles, there is no wind. Still no clue how the future plan for the people looks like.
