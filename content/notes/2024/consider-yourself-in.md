Consider yourself in a wildfire, casually enjoying the fire. Breathe. That'll be a fine particle, aka PM 2.5 #airpollution of ~1000. Translated to the Air Quality Index, it'll be an #AQI of ~2000. 

[#Delhi](https://www.nytimes.com/2024/11/19/world/asia/india-delhi-air-pollution.html?smid=nytcore-android-share) recently had an AQI of over *1,700*. 

It's spiralling.

5 is healthy, 100 dangerous, 300 hazardous.
